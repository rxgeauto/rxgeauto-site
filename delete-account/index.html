Delete your RXGE Auto account

You can delete your account and its data at any time.

In the app

Open RXGE Auto, go to Profile → Edit Profile → Delete Account, then confirm. Your account is deleted straight away.

Without the app

Email support@rxgeauto.com from the email address you registered with, using the subject Delete my account. We may ask you to confirm it is your account. We will delete it within 7 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account and profile
  • Your cars, service logs, modifications and reminders
  • Vehicle document details you entered, such as registration, insurance and NTSA dates

What may be kept

Payment records, if you ever made a purchase, may be kept for as long as the law requires. Backups, where kept, are overwritten on a rolling basis.

Active subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription bought through Google Play. Cancel it in the Google Play Store under Payments & subscriptions before you delete your account.
